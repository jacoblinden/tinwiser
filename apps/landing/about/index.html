About Us

Focused. Rigorous.
Built for industry.

Tinwiser works where smart manufacturing, IIoT, digital twins, AI in manufacturing, and autonomous operations meet — with a team that prefers proof, partnership, and long horizons over noise.

Our Story

Born from a simple question.

What if plants could adopt IIoT, twins, and industrial AI without seven-year programs — and still keep safety and uptime non-negotiable?

That tension is Tinwiser’s starting point. We’re a small team with roots in software, data, and physical systems — convinced that smart manufacturing and autonomous operations win when vendors stop overselling and start co-investing with operators.

Our strength is concentration: knowing where we’re strongest, where partners add irreplaceable domain knowledge, and how to sequence work so outcomes hold up in production. Capital and collaboration follow when that alignment is clear.

What Drives Us

Our values shape our work.

Integrity

Precision in how we describe scope and progress — especially in manufacturing, where trust is earned in outages and audits, not taglines.

Craft

When we commit to a use case, we care about reliability, integration pain, and operators’ time — not demo polish alone.

Impact

Industrial work has consequences. We want outcomes that show up in OEE, scrap, energy, or safety — not vanity metrics.
