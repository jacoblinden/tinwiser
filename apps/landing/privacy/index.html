Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website, tinwiser.com, is operated by Tinwiser. Questions about this policy or your personal data can be sent to hello@tinwiser.com.

What this site collects

This is a static informational website. It has no user accounts, no forms, no login, and does not ask for passwords or payment details. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Like any web server, our hosting provider (Google Cloud) records standard technical request logs, such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time, for security and operations. These logs are not used to identify or profile visitors.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

When you email us

If you contact us by email, we use your name, email address and message only to respond and to continue the conversation you started. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to its use. Email hello@tinwiser.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
